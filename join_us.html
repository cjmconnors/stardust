On Stage

Come Perform With Us

You don't need a résumé. You don't need to have done this before. Stardust auditions are open to anyone with a desire to be on stage — newcomers and veterans alike.

Our productions span the full range of performance art: classic drama, musical theater, original works, spoken word, and more. Whatever form of performance speaks to you, there is a place for it here.

We believe in casting people, not types. Come as you are. Let us see what you've got.

What to expect at auditions

  • Open to all experience levels — no prior theater experience required
  • Sides (script excerpts) shared in advance so you can prepare
  • Musical auditions include a brief song — guidance provided
  • A welcoming, low-pressure environment — we want you to succeed
  • Crew and production roles available for those who prefer offstage

Behind the Curtain

Join the Crew

Theater is a team sport. For every actor on stage, there are a dozen people making the magic possible. No prior experience required — just a willingness to learn and show up.

🪚
Set Construction

Build the worlds our stories live in. If you can swing a hammer or pick up a paintbrush, you're exactly who we need.

💡
Lighting & Sound

Control the atmosphere. Lighting and sound design are among the most powerful storytelling tools in theater.

🪡
Costumes & Wardrobe

Bring characters to life through what they wear. Sewing skills welcome but not required — lots of roles here.

📋
Stage Management

Keep the show running smoothly. Stage managers are the organized backbone of every production.

🎨
Props & Set Dressing

Source, build, and manage the objects that populate our stage. Creative problem-solving at its best.

📸
Photography & Media

Capture the moments and help us share our story with the community through photography and social media.

Give to Stardust

As a nonprofit organization, Stardust Community Players depends on the generosity of people who believe the arts make a community stronger. Every contribution — large or small — goes directly to our work.

Your support pays for production costs, costumes, set materials, venue rental, and programs that make theater accessible to everyone in our community.

Interested in sponsoring a specific production, event, or program? We'd love to talk.
