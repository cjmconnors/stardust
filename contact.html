Where to Find Us

Rooted in Danvers,
Open to All

Stardust Community Players is based in Danvers, Massachusetts, and serves the broader North Shore community. We are a traveling theater — our rehearsals, events, and performances take place at various venues throughout the area.

Specific venue details are announced with each event. Sign up for our mailing list and you'll always know exactly where to find us.

A note on venues: We are actively seeking a long-term performance home in the Danvers area. If you have a space to suggest or offer, we'd love to hear from you.
